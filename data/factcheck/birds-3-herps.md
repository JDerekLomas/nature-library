# Fact-check: birds-south-seas.json (52) + herps.json (53)

Date: 2026-10-18. 105 entries checked. 23 field edits across 21 entries.

**Limits of this check:** the session's shared web-search budget ran out partway through. About 30 targeted claims were checked against web sources (listed below). The rest were checked against standard references from memory: Birds of the World / BirdLife factsheets, IUCN categories, the Reptile Database, AmphibiaWeb and WHO snakebite guidance. Those are marked "unchanged (reviewed)". Re-verify them with live sources if a stricter audit is needed.

Format: species — field — wrong → now — source

## First-aid / venom review (herps)
All venomous entries were checked against WHO guidance: keep the person still, immobilise the limb, remove rings, get to hospital fast; no cutting, sucking, tourniquets or ice.
- Pressure-immobilisation is advised only for elapids (black mamba, inland taipan, sea krait) and is explicitly not advised for vipers (Russell's, Gaboon, saw-scaled, terciopelo). Correct as written.
- No lookalike text encourages handling. The coral snake entry already says "Never handle"; the boomslang wording was fixed (above).
- No unsafe statements found.
