# Fact-check log: data/codex/medicinal.json

Entries checked: 59. Changes: 23 (in 19 entries).

Method: I checked IUCN status for all 59 entries against the IUCN Red List checklist mirrored on GBIF (dataset 19491596-35ae-4a91-9a98-85cf505f1bd3, https://api.gbif.org/v1/species/search?datasetKey=19491596-35ae-4a91-9a98-85cf505f1bd3). The other fields were checked against Wikipedia, EMA, Grieve's *A Modern Herbal* and existing domain knowledge. The session's web-search quota ran out partway through, so later checks used direct page fetches only. Claims marked "not independently re-verified" below were kept because they match well-established sources, but they were not fetched this session.
