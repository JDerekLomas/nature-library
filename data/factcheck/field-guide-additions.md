# Field-guide fields added

Date: 2026-10-18. Files: `data/codex/wings.json` (52 entries) and `data/codex/creatures.json` (62 entries). All 114 are Animalia and none had field-guide fields before; all 114 received some. No existing field was changed; entry count and order were checked after writing.

**Sources.** Web search was not used. Entries marked [web] were checked this session against the linked Wikipedia page (Prion Island's closure dates came from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prion_Island). The rest were written from standard references (Wikipedia species pages, Cornell's Birds of the World / All About Birds, BirdLife DataZone, Animal Diversity Web, FishBase) and should be confirmed at the Wikipedia URL given. Fields listed as "omitted" were left out on purpose. Either they don't apply (e.g. `voice` for silent animals, `whereToSee` for deep-sea or inaccessible species), or they couldn't be confirmed this session (e.g. the Raggiana bird-of-paradise's call, a named site for the king cobra after the 2024 species split, and look-alikes for the Menelaus morpho and desert locust).

**Safety.** Look-alike text for dangerous animals (big cats, wolf, elephant, rhino, hippo, bears, bison, honey badger, wild dog, crocodile, Komodo dragon, king cobra, great white, ostrich, platypus, poison frog, mantis shrimp, flying fox) tells readers to keep their distance or not to handle the animal.
